# 📚 Chapter 46: Spatial Autocorrelation & Tobler's First Law

## 46.1 Theoretical Overview
Tobler's First Law of Geography states: Everything is related to everything else, but near things are more related than distant things.

Moran's I statistic measures spatial clustering: I = (N/S0) * [Σ Σ w_ij (x_i - x_bar)(x_j - x_bar)] / [Σ (x_i - x_bar)^2].
